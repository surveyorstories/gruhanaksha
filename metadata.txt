    • Parcel & Map Production: Automated PPM/LPM generation, atlas exporting, and auto LP numbering.
    • Survey & Traverse Plotting: Traverse plotter, FMB plotter, point/CSV coordinate importer, and KMZ integration.
    • Precision Geometry Editing: Polygon adjuster, polygon splitter, AutoCAD-style aligner, freehand adjuster, trim & extend, and topology verification.
    • Data Protection & Recovery: Automatic background backups, auto-save, and accidental crash data recovery.
    • Styling & Workflow Tools: Pre-built symbology styles and automated processing algorithms.
